# Guided selection UX · 2026-10-06

Goal: reach a banking option without filling a document-preparation workspace first.

Implemented: legal form and five plain goal checkboxes come first. A goal is a preference; essential requirements, currencies and payment volume are disclosed separately. Company context and evidence are collapsed but available before assessment. Selection uses the same company profile and validated needs, without changing eligibility rules. Choosing an option opens preparation and selects its route. Bank follow-up remains collapsed separately. Reload restores saved needs; expanding a section does not modify data.

The baseline remains a fictional company. Real customers and documents are not supported. Capability coverage is still incomplete; prices and reviews are not part of ranking. A positive match is not a promise of bank acceptance.

Acceptance: first load hides company fields and follow-up; selecting a goal updates the corresponding preference; advanced priorities stay synchronized; chosen route opens preparation; saved goals survive reload; desktop and mobile do not overflow horizontally. `tests/guided-ux-e2e.mjs` failed before implementation and passes after it. Existing workflow tests explicitly reveal their relevant workspace sections.
